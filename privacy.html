Privacy Policy — Node Health Report

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Who we are

Node Health Report ("the App") is operated by Exploratus Digital LLC ("we", "us"), reachable at knowaguy4u@gmail.com.

What the App does

The App runs in Pi Browser and gives Pi Node operators a plain-words health report for their node: what is running, what is stale, and what to fix first. It presents checks for node status, blockchain sync, and connectivity, and ranks the fixes by impact.

What data the App collects

  • No server-side collection. The App does not operate a backend that receives or stores your node data, system information, or report contents.
  • No tracking. The App sets no cookies and includes no analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.
  • Reports are yours. Anything the App shows you stays in your browser session unless you choose to share it through your own channels.

Pi account information

When you sign in with Pi Authentication, Pi provides the App with your Pi username, and with your wallet address only where you approve that scope. That information is used only to label your session and your reports inside the App. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Because the App keeps no server-side copy of your information, there is nothing for us to retain or erase. Closing the App ends the session.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address, with a new "Last updated" date, before the change takes effect.
