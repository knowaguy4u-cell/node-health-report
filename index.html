        WHAT WE CAN'T SEE

        We probe your node's public ports from our server and compare its ledger with the public network record. Nothing is installed on your computer, and we never ask you to install anything.

        A check is a snapshot from the moment it runs, taken while this page is open. We do not watch in the background and we cannot send alerts.

        Peers, Docker containers, CPU, memory, disk, and your node's own software version live on your machine. Tools that show them ask you to install software on the node computer. We don't — so we don't show those numbers, and we never guess them.

        The health score is computed only from the ports and the sync gap we actually measured. When something can't be measured, the app says so instead of estimating.
